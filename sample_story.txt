Deep within the mystical green forest, a hidden stone temple stood surrounded by ancient mossy trees. Golden sunlight filtered through the dense leaves, illuminating dust particles dancing in the quiet air. A stone fountain at the center slowly dripped crystal clear water, creating soft rings of ripples.

Suddenly, a majestic white tiger stepped out of the shadows. Its blue eyes sparkled with curiosity as it walked slowly toward the dripping stone fountain. The tiger lowered its head, drinking the cool water, its ears twitching at the sound of the forest.

With a sudden shift, the white tiger looked up toward the sky as a large golden butterfly fluttered near its nose. The tiger blinked, watching the glowing insect fly away toward the sunlight, casting a warm shadow on the ancient temple ground.
